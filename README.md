# Free Habit Tracker With No Streak Guilt

**▶ Use it live: https://pittner.github.io/free-habit-tracker-no-streak-guilt/**

A free, private, no-signup habit tracker that **forgives your missed days**. One bad day never resets your progress to zero, there's no red X of shame, and it's built for brains that don't run on perfect chains (hi, ADHD). Everything is stored locally in your browser — nothing is ever uploaded.

## What it does

- ✍️ Add habits with a friendly icon
- ✅ Tap any day in a 5-week grid to check in (or hit "Mark today done")
- 🌱 **Kind streak** — a streak that *survives one missed day* instead of crashing to zero
- 📈 **Days shown up** — a total that only ever goes up and can never be taken away
- 🔁 **Comebacks** — returning after a gap is counted as a win, because it's the actual skill
- ⬜ Missed days are neutral grey, **never red**
- 🔒 100% client-side — no account, no server, no tracking; export your data as JSON anytime

## Why forgiving?

Most habit apps are built on punishment: string together a streak, miss one day, watch the counter reset to `0` in red, then avoid the app out of guilt. That's not a willpower problem — it's a design problem. The missed day is the most useful moment there is (it tells you what gets in your way), so this tool keeps your progress visible after a slip and makes coming back feel easy instead of shameful.
